About Power Learn Project

The Power Learn Project (PLP) is a Pan-African non-profit organization committed to empowering young Africans with technology skills.

Founded in 2022, PLP aims to drive transformative change across the continent through digital skills training.

PLP's Impact on African Tech Education

PLP has already trained over 10,000 young Africans in various tech disciplines.

  • Providing accessible tech education
  • Bridging the digital skills gap
  • Creating employment opportunities

Future Goals

PLP aims to train 1 million young Africans by 2027, focusing on:

  1. Software Development
  2. Data Science
  3. Blockchain Technology
